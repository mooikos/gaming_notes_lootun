# Progression & Endgame

## Confirmed Dungeon Progression Log (Phase 1)

- **Active Rule**: Switch immediately to every newly unlocked Mission / Dungeon from `Lv. 1 -> 100`. See [Items by Monster Level Band & Dungeon](items-crafting/by-level) for deduplicated base tiers and boss drops.

| # | Dungeon / Area | Level Stayed | Unlocks Next At | Key Progression Milestones |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | `Woodland` | `Lv. 1 -> 5` | `Lv. 5` | Starter `Lv. 1–14` bases (`Leather` Medium set, `Basic Hammer`); `Wolf Pelt Cloak`, `Brutes Warhammer` |
| 2 | `Graveyard` | `Lv. 5 -> 11` | `Lv. 11` | `Bone Bow` (Ranger `2H`), `Reavers Shield`; `Copper` accessories begin at `Lv. 10+` |
| 3 | `Deep Forest` | `Lv. 11 -> 17` | `Lv. 17` | Crosses `Lv. 15` threshold (opens `Lv. 15–49` `Cloth`/`Rough`/`Iron` bases & `Iron` accessories); `Orcish Charm`, `Queens Eye`, `Spider Silk Wraps`, `Bear Claw` |
| 4 | `Orc Camp` | `Lv. 17 -> 24` | `Lv. 24` | Full `Lv. 15–49` drop band active; exclusives: `Bonecleaver`, `Berserker Helm`, `Cloak of Purity` |
| 5 | `Goblin Camp` | `Lv. 24 -> 31` | `Lv. 31` | Continues `Lv. 15–49` band; exclusives: `Mages Shawl`, `Goblin Tooth Necklace`, `Ring of Manipulation` (`Divine`) |
| 6 | `Rock Cave` | `Lv. 31 -> 38` | `Lv. 38` | Continues `Lv. 15–49` band; exclusives: `Rockwall` (`Divine` Shield), `Stone Carapace`, `Frost Ring`, `Flame Ring` |
| 7 | `Scorched Plains` | `Lv. 38 -> 45` | `Lv. 45` | Continues `Lv. 15–49` band; exclusives: `Desert Tarsus` (`2H` Polearm), `Scorched Bow` (`Divine` Bow), `Wurm Carapace`, `Royal Cuffs` |
| 8 | `Frigid Wasteland` | `Lv. 45 -> 52` | `Lv. 52` | Crosses `Lv. 50` threshold (opens `Lv. 50–99` `Woven` Light, `Heavy` Medium, & `Lead` Heavy/Weapon/Accessory band); exclusives: `Frost Sting` (`1H` Sword), `Yeti Hide Hood`, `Frozen Talisman` (`Divine`) |
| 9 | `Jungle Ruins` | `Lv. 52 -> 60` | `Lv. 60` | Continues `Lv. 50–99` band (`Lead Hammer` & `Lead Spear` confirmed); exclusives: `Beetle Blade`, `Ritual Knife`, `Living Sash`, `Jungle Cape` (`Heavy`), `Storm Band`, `Lightning Talisman` (`Divine`) |
