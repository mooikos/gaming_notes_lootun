# Classes & Ascendancies

Characters start as one of **4 Base Classes** and unlock **3 Ascendancy specializations** at **Level 100** (granting 50 additional Ascendancy levels and specialized passive trees).

## Class & Ascendancy Matrix

| Base Class | Archetype Focus | Ascendancy 1 | Ascendancy 2 | Ascendancy 3 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Warrior** | High defense, melee/physical combat | `Barbarian` | `Juggernaut` | `Warden` (Block Tank) |
| **Ranger** | High attack speed, mobility & ranged DPS | `Assassin` | `Marksman` | `Renegade` (Evasion Tank) |
| **Mage** | Elemental & arcane spell scaling | `Archmage` | `Vizier` | `Battlemage` (Barrier Tank) |
| **Paladin** | Holy blessings, mitigation, thorns & DoTs | `Templar` (Block/Thorns Tank) | `Crusader` (Raid Buffer/DPS) | `Inquisitor` (Ignite/DoT) |
