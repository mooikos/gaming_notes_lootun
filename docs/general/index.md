# General

Overview of **Lootun**'s core combat, skill, base-building, and gathering systems.

## Combat & Skills

- **Default Attacks**: Automatically triggered attacks requiring a matching weapon type; scale with **Weapon Damage %** and **Attack Time** (attack speed).
- **Cooldown Skills**: Active offensive, defensive, and utility skills automated through **Barracks** upgrades.
- **Skill Masteries**: Each skill can gain up to **20 Mastery Levels** to unlock specialized passive modifiers, target count increases, or damage conversions.
- **Defense Layers**: Armor, Elemental Resistances, Block (`Warden` / `Templar`), Dodge/Evasion (`Renegade`), Barrier (`Battlemage` / `Templar`), Leech, and Thorns.

## Base Buildings & Professions

- **Scrapper**: Dismantles unwanted loot into crafting materials; upgrades automate salvaging by rarity tier.
- **Blacksmith**: Crafts, upgrades, and enhances equipment.
- **Barracks**: Unlocks automated skill casting, cooldown priority rules, and targeting configuration.
- **Artisan**: Transmutes attributes and supports late-game gear optimization.
- **Profession Hall**: Unlocks passive resource buildings (**Mine**, **Forest**, **Farm**) scaled via worker slots, cycle speed upgrades, and monster-dropped **Profession Tools**.
- **In-Game Glossary**: Tracks discovered monsters, damage profiles, abilities, and exact item drop locations for target-farming.
